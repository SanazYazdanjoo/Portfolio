BL (Blendedkurs)

A note on the visual treatment —
avatars are initial monograms rather than stock photographs. A stock face on an unvalidated persona implies a specificity the research does not yet support; the monogram keeps the board honest about what it is. The ring colour marks the persona's role (matching the role palette); its line style tracks confidence: solid for validated, dashed for unvalidated or to confirm, faint dotted for hypothesis-only.
